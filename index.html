Our Story

Three generations of woven artistry, brought to Charlotte.

Pineville Rug Gallery was founded in 1986 on a simple belief: that a great rug is not only décor, but also an heirloom. Owner Reza Elyasi is a third-generation rug dealer whose sister owned a rug factory in Tehran, and his family brings a century of knowledge, importing breathtaking hand-knotted pieces from Isfahan, Tabriz, Kashan, and Heriz.

Every rug is chosen for quality, durability, and authenticity, then cared for by hand in our own gallery. It's craftsmanship you can feel underfoot — and hand down through generations, often lasting more than a hundred years.

Cleaning · Repair · Appraisal

Care worthy of an heirloom.

Whether you bought it from us or inherited it generations ago, our master craftsmen treat every rug as if it were their own — with complimentary local pickup & delivery.

01

Rug Cleaning

Hand-washed the way fine rugs demand — vacuumed, dust drawn from front and back, gently hand-washed, then dried in open air. Never harsh-machined, so delicate wool and silk keep their color and luster.

02

Repair & Restoration

Fraying edges, moth damage, tears, worn areas — even fringe caught and shredded by a household vacuum, the most common mishap. Every repair is hand-sewn by our in-house expert and quoted in person.

Book Repair →
03

Appraisal

Uncover the true value and origin of your rug with four decades of expertise. A verbal appraisal is complimentary; a printed certificate — mailed and emailed as a PDF — is $250, with a discount for multiple pieces.
