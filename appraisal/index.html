What is your rug worth? Let's find out.

Whether it's a family heirloom or a rug you've always wondered about, our team brings four decades of experience to tell you where it came from and what it's worth.

OptionWhat you getPrice

When a written appraisal helps

  • Insurance. Make sure a valuable rug is covered for what it's really worth.
  • Estates and inheritances. Know what you have before deciding what to do with it.
  • Selling or donating. Start from a real number.
  • Peace of mind. Sometimes you just want to know.

What we look at

Where the rug was made, how old it is, what it's made of, how it was woven, and its condition. Four decades of handling fine rugs tell us a lot.
