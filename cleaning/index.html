How we clean your rug

  1. We pick it up. Free local pickup and delivery, so you never have to roll and haul a heavy rug.
  2. We get the dirt out first. Every rug is vacuumed, and dust is drawn out from both the front and the back.
  3. We wash it by hand. Gently, with care for the dyes and fibers. Never harsh-machined.
  4. We dry it in open air. Slow, natural drying that is kind to wool and silk.
  5. We bring it home. Clean, fresh, and ready for your floor.

Why hand-washing matters

Hand-knotted rugs are made with natural wool, silk and vegetable dyes. A regular carpet cleaner's hot water and strong chemicals can make colors run, shrink the rug, or leave the pile stiff. Washing by hand keeps the color and shine your rug was made with, so it lasts for generations.
